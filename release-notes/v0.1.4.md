Totufoto 0.1.4 brings a reworked Photos tab, faster face grouping for large libraries, zoom in the viewer, and layouts for phones and tablets.

## Downloads

- **Windows 10/11**: `Totufoto-0.1.4-windows-x64.exe`. Double-click it. It is not signed, so the first time Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux (x86-64)**: `Totufoto-0.1.4-linux-x86_64.AppImage`. Run `chmod +x Totufoto-*.AppImage`, then start it. If it says FUSE is missing, install `libfuse2` or run it with `--appimage-extract-and-run`.

Your folders, people names and index carry over from earlier versions; nothing is indexed again.

## What's new

### Photos
- Grouped photos (by year, month, day or place) show as cards; click one to see its photos, close its chip to go back.
- The cards load from a short summary instead of every photo, so large libraries open much faster.
- New **date range** filter (From / to).
- The **Places** tab is replaced by grouping by place, sorted by most photos, with a **No location** card.
- Filters respond at once, with placeholders while photos load; the bar with the chips and **Clear all** stays at the top while scrolling.
- One chip per selected person, each with its own ×.

### People
- A compact **List** layout next to Cards, with a size slider, and sorting by **Most photos** or **Name**.
- Rename people from the sidebar or from the photo viewer, including unnamed groups and faces in no group.
- Names are unique: using an existing name offers to **merge** or to keep them apart as "Name (1)".
- **Not them** moves a face to a new group you can name; **Same as…** in the viewer moves a single face to someone.
- The People tab is much faster and no longer flickers with thousands of people.
- Named people are remembered even when all their photos are deleted, and their faces rejoin them when the photos come back.

### Photo viewer
- Click or tap to zoom, drag to move, and zoom with the mouse wheel or a pinch.
- No size jump while the full photo loads, and no arrow where there is no next or previous photo.
- Opening a photo whose file was deleted tells you so and removes it from the gallery (unless its whole folder is offline).

### Faces and indexing
- After a scan only the new faces are grouped, which takes a fraction of a second instead of minutes on large libraries. **Regroup all faces** in Settings does a full regroup.
- Files that can't be read are listed in Settings with the reason, skipped until they change, and can be retried.

### Layout
- **Settings** (photo folders, indexing progress, rescan) behind the gear at the top right.
- The **People in the photo** column can be collapsed.
- Layouts for **phones and tablets**: a people panel that slides in, dates and grouping in a panel, and swipe and pinch in the viewer.
