Totufoto 0.1.9 makes the Back button work the way you expect, and lets the photo details fold away.

## Downloads

- **Windows 10/11**: `Totufoto-0.1.9-windows-x64.exe`. Double-click it. It is not signed, so the first time Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux (x86-64)**: `Totufoto-0.1.9-linux-x86_64.AppImage`. Run `chmod +x Totufoto-*.AppImage`, then start it. If it says FUSE is missing, install `libfuse2` or run it with `--appimage-extract-and-run`.

Your folders, people names and index carry over from earlier versions; nothing is indexed again.

## What's new

### Back button (in the browser, especially on phones)
- Back undoes one step at a time instead of leaving the gallery: it closes an open dialog, the open photo (you stay where its thumbnail is) or a panel, then clears the filter chips, then goes back to Photos from another tab.
- With nothing left to undo, it asks **Leave Totufoto?** with **Stay** and **Leave**.
- Browsers only allow this after you have touched the page once, so pressing Back right after opening the gallery still leaves it.

### Photo details
- A button at the top right of the details (the same icon as the **People in the photo** column) folds them away to give the photo more room; on phones they fold down below the photo. The `I` key does the same, and the choice is remembered.
