# Code of conduct

Imadive is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place for everyone who uses it or helps with it, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before you judge.
- Disagree about ideas, never about people. Explain your reasons.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it so that it helps.
- Keep discussions on topic, and respect other people's time.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes and language, or sexual content and attention.
- Publishing other people's private information (including photos) without their permission.
- Trolling, or repeatedly disrupting discussions.

## Where it applies

In the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, write to the maintainer at waitingtimeout@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, lock discussions, or block people who don't follow this code of conduct, and will explain why when that is safe.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
