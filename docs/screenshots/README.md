# Screenshots

The README's screenshots, taken by `web/tests/screenshots.mjs` (Playwright) of a gallery on the library described here:

```sh
cd web/tests && npm ci && npx playwright install chromium
node screenshots.mjs http://127.0.0.1:7878
```

## The photos

77 colour photographs from the Farm Security Administration and Office of War Information collection of the US Library of Congress, taken from 1940 to 1943, most of them by Russell Lee in Pie Town, New Mexico. They are in the **public domain** (works of the US government, with no known restrictions on publication), copied from Wikimedia Commons at 1600 pixels wide.

To show the gallery as it is used, each photo was given EXIF data:

- **Date**: the month of the Library of Congress record, on the 1st at noon (the records give no day or time).
- **Place**: the coordinates of the town named in the record. The gallery shows the nearest place with more than 1,000 inhabitants, so Pie Town appears as Alamo.
- **People**: the faces were found and grouped by the gallery itself. Four groups were named after the people the photos' captions name, after checking the faces: Jack Whinery and Mrs. Whinery, Jim Norris, and Mrs. Bill Stagg. The other groups are left unnamed.
